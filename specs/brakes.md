## Pad Replacement Notes

- Front pads wear faster than rears - expect to replace fronts 2-3x for every rear change
- Always replace pads in axle pairs (both sides at once)
- Compress caliper pistons carefully - rear pistons must be wound back (clockwise) due to the integrated parking brake mechanism. A caliper wind-back tool is required for the rears
- Check discs for lip, scoring, and minimum thickness when changing pads
- Bed in new pads with 20-30 moderate stops from ~50 km/h, avoiding heavy braking for the first 200 km

## Disc Replacement Notes

- Discs should be replaced in axle pairs
- New discs often have a protective coating (wax/oil) that must be cleaned off with brake cleaner before fitting
- Hub mating surface must be clean and rust-free for proper disc seating
- Caliper carrier bolts: torque to manufacturer spec (check Haynes manual)

## Parking Brake

- Cable-operated drum-in-disc type on rear wheels
- Self-adjusting mechanism - if the handbrake travel becomes excessive, the drums may need cleaning/adjustment
- Cable can seize if the car sits unused - operate regularly
