### Towing Notes
- The Mondeo Estate is a popular tow car but the 2.2 TDCi's torque makes it one of the better options
- Ensure tow bar is rated and properly wired (13-pin or twin 7-pin)
- Fuse F74 (20A) and F92 (20A) in the passenger compartment box serve the trailer connector
- Extended mirrors are legally required if the trailer is wider than the car

## Titanium X Standard Equipment

The Titanium X was the top-of-range Mk3 trim. Key standard equipment:

**Exterior:**
- 18" 7-spoke alloy wheels
- Black roof bars (estate)
- Body colour bumpers with chrome trim
- Chrome mesh grille with chrome surround
- Front fog lights
- Automatic headlights
- Privacy glass (rear windows)
- Electric folding heated mirrors with puddle lights
- Rain sensor wipers

**Interior:**
- Alcantara/leather upholstery
- Variable climate-controlled front seats (heated and cooled)
- Leather steering wheel with metal-finish spokes
- Leather gear knob with satin aluminium insert
- Electronic Automatic Temperature Control (EATC / dual-zone climate)
- Cruise control
- Auto-dimming rear view mirror
- Multi-function trip computer
- Stainless steel door scuff plates
- Analogue clock with chrome surround

**Comfort:**
- Quickclear heated front windscreen
- Heated rear window
- Electric front windows (one-touch up/down with bounce-back)
- Electric rear windows with global closing
- Reach and rake adjustable steering column
- Centre console with armrest/storage and cupholder

**Safety (standard on Titanium X):**
- ESP (Electronic Stability Programme)
- EBA (Emergency Brake Assist)
- ABS with EBD
- Dual-stage driver and passenger airbags
- Front side airbags
- Front curtain airbags
- Front seatbelt pre-tensioners
- Perimeter alarm with volumetric sensing
- Safeguard immobiliser

**Options fitted on this car:**
- Xenon headlamps with auto levelling
- 8-way electric driver's seat
- Park assist (parking sensors)
- Bluetooth hands-free phone kit
- Electric sunroof
- Heavy duty battery
- Sony stereo radio / 6-disc CD dash mounted autochanger

**Not fitted:**
- CD/DVD navigation system
- Heated rear seats
