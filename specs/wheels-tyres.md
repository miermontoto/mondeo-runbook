# Wheels and Tyres

## Wheel Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bolt pattern (PCD) | 5x108 |
| Centre bore | 63.4 mm |
| Lug nut thread | M12 x 1.5 |
| Lug nut torque | 115-142 Nm (85-105 ft-lbs) |
| Lug nut type | Conical seat (60-degree taper) |

### Currently Fitted: Ford Focus RS Rims

This car runs **Ford Focus RS wheels** (from the ~2002-2005 era) instead of the original Titanium X alloys.
These are a direct fit - same 5x108 PCD and 63.4mm centre bore as the Mondeo.

| Parameter | Focus RS Mk1 (2002-2003) | Focus ST225 Mk2 (2005+) |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 18x8J | 18x8J |
| Offset | ET 52-55 | ET 55 |
| PCD | 5x108 | 5x108 |
| Centre bore | 63.4 mm | 63.4 mm |
| Tyre | 225/40 R18 | 225/40 R18 |
| Design | OZ Racing multi-spoke | 5-spoke "snowflake" |

Both RS and ST wheels are within safe offset range for the Mondeo (factory ET 52).
The slightly higher offset of the ST wheels tucks them in marginally more - no rubbing issues.

### Original Titanium X Wheel (not currently fitted)
- 18" 7-spoke alloy (Titanium X design), 7Jx18 ET52

### Spare Wheel
- Check what came with the car: 16" steel full-size spare OR mini space-saver
- If running 18" wheels with a 16" spare, the spare has a different rolling radius - limit speed and distance

### Notes on Sizing
- The 225/40 R18 tyres on the Focus RS rims are the same spec as the Titanium X originals - no speedo or ABS calibration concerns
- Lower profile 18" tyres are more vulnerable to pothole damage and cost more to replace than 16" or 17" alternatives
- A set of 16" steel wheels with winter tyres is a popular and cheap option for cold months
- When replacing tyres, always fit at least two matching tyres on the same axle
- The speed rating must meet or exceed the original specification

## Tyre Pressures

Tyre pressures are listed on a sticker on the driver's door jamb (B-pillar area).
Typical values for the 2.2 TDCi estate (these are approximate - always check the door sticker):

| Load Condition | Front | Rear |
|---|---|---|
| Normal load (205/55 R16) | ~2.3 bar (33 psi) | ~2.5 bar (36 psi) |
| Full load (205/55 R16) | ~2.5 bar (36 psi) | ~2.9 bar (42 psi) |

### Notes
- Check pressures when tyres are cold (not driven for 2+ hours or less than 3 km)
- The estate typically runs slightly higher rear pressures than the hatch/saloon
- Increase rear pressure when carrying heavy loads or towing
- Don't forget to check the spare tyre pressure periodically

## Spare Wheel

- The estate may come with a full-size steel spare or a space-saver (varies by market/spec)
- If space-saver: max speed 80 km/h, drive to nearest tyre shop only
- The spare is located under the boot floor

## Wheel Compatibility

The 5x108 / 63.4mm centre bore pattern is shared with:
- Other Ford models (Focus Mk2/3, C-Max, S-Max, Galaxy, Kuga)
- Volvo (S60, V70, S80 - same centre bore)
- Jaguar X-Type
- Some Peugeot/Citroen models (but different centre bore - requires spigot rings)

Always verify offset (ET) is within 5mm of factory spec to avoid rubbing or excessive bearing wear.
