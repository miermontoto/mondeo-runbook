# Engine - 2.2 Duratorq TDCi (ZSD-422)

## Overview

The Duratorq ZSD-422 is a Ford-designed 2.2L inline-4 turbodiesel, introduced in 2004 for the top-spec
Mondeo and Jaguar X-Type. It is **not** related to the PSA DW12 2.2 engine used in Peugeots/Citroens.
Later variants powered the Ford Transit (2006-2016), Land Rover Defender (2011-2016), and Ford Ranger T6.

## Timing System

- **Camshaft drive:** Timing chain (not belt)
- **Replacement interval:** No fixed interval - Ford considers it a "lifetime" component
- **Inspection:** Check chain tension at ~150,000 km; replace if slack, noisy, or tensioner is failing
- **Tensioner replacement interval:** ~150,000 km recommended
- If the chain skips, catastrophic engine damage is likely (interference engine)

## Turbocharger

- Variable geometry turbo (VGT) with electronic actuator
- The actuator is the most failure-prone component on this engine
- Actuator part numbers on the unit: 6NW-008-412 (black top), G221 or G149 (aluminium side)
- Ford only sells the actuator as part of a complete turbo assembly (very expensive)
- Aftermarket repair/remanufacture of the actuator alone is possible and much cheaper

## Auxiliary Drive

- Serpentine belt drives alternator, A/C compressor, and power steering pump
- **Replacement interval:** ~160,000 km (100,000 miles)
- A broken auxiliary belt will disable the alternator but steering and cooling continue to function
- The water pump is driven by the timing chain, not the auxiliary belt
