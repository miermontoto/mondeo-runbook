### Notes
- The smart charging system varies alternator output based on load - a standard lead-acid battery will not charge correctly
- Always disconnect the negative terminal first when removing, reconnect it last when installing
- After reconnecting, the car may need to relearn idle, window positions, and radio code
- This car has the heavy duty battery option (listed as a Titanium X option) - confirm spec when replacing

## Troubleshooting Tips

- If something electrical stops working, always check the fuse first
- Keep a selection of spare fuses in the car (especially 7.5A, 15A, 20A)
- If a fuse blows repeatedly, there is a short circuit - do not simply fit a higher-rated fuse
- The cigarette lighter fuse (F68) is a common one to check when 12V accessories stop working
- Central locking issues: check F24 (20A) in the engine bay

## This Car's Specific Equipment

### Xenon Headlights (with auto-levelling)
- Uses 20A fuses at F16 (left) and F18 (right) - NOT the 7.5A halogen fuses
- Xenon ballasts are mounted behind the headlight units
- Bulb type: D2S xenon (do NOT touch the bulb glass with bare fingers)
- Auto-levelling motors on each headlight adjust beam height based on vehicle load
- If one headlight fails to ignite: check fuse first, then ballast, then bulb (in that order)
- Xenon bulbs dim gradually over time - replace in pairs to maintain even lighting
- The auto-levelling system must work correctly to pass ITV - misaimed xenons are a fail point

### Sony 6-CD Autochanger
- Head unit: Sony stereo radio with dash-mounted 6-disc CD changer
- Fuses: F69 (7.5A, instrument cluster/audio), F72 (20A, radio)
- 4 speakers standard (Titanium X)
- Uses Ford-specific wiring harness - aftermarket head units need an adapter loom and steering wheel control adapter
- If the CD changer jams: hold eject, or disconnect the battery for 30 seconds to reset

### Park Assist (Parking Sensors)
- Rear parking sensors fitted (Titanium X option)
- If sensors give false readings: check for dirt/ice on the sensor faces, or a faulty sensor (they can be replaced individually)

### Electric Sunroof
- Fuse: F73 (20A)
- If it stops working: check fuse, then try manual override (there may be an Allen key slot for emergency close)
- Keep the drain channels clear to prevent water ingress into the cabin

### Bluetooth Hands-Free
- Factory-fitted advanced Bluetooth phone kit
- Pairs with most modern phones for calls
- Audio streaming capability is limited/absent on this era of Bluetooth - use an AUX adapter or FM transmitter for music
