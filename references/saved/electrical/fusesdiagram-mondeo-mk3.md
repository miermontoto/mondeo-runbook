## Fuse Box Locations

### Passenger Compartment Fuse Box

**Left-hand drive:** Located behind the storage compartment underneath the instrument panel. Open the storage compartment and pull outward to access.

**Right-hand drive:** Situated behind the glove compartment. Open the glove box, push the lever at the back to the left, press the sides inward, and pull out.

### Engine Compartment Fuse Box

Pull off the battery cover, then remove the fuse box cover by releasing the catch and lifting up.

## Model Year Notes

The documentation covers all years 2000-2007 with notations for variations between early (pre-07/02 and pre-01/02) and later (post-08/02 and post-02/02) model updates, particularly affecting:
- Daytime running lamps configurations (F84 vs F88)
- Diesel engine-specific fuses (F33)
- Heated windscreen relay timing (F27)
