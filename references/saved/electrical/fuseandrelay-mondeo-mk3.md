## Fuse Box Locations

### Engine Compartment
The fuse and relay box is located on the left side next to the headlight and is covered by a protective cover. The reverse side of the cover displays the element designations.

### Passenger Compartment
Located on the driver's side, under the dashboard, behind the glove compartment.
