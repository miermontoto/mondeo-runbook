## Overview

The Ford Mondeo Mk3 (second generation) was unveiled in October 2000 and represented a significant size increase compared to its predecessor. It was considerably larger than its predecessor and marked their first vehicle to fully benefit from the Prodigy concept car.

The design represented a departure from Ford's New Edge philosophy, offering what critics described as more restrained and mature, if much less distinctive. Key improvements included a 50mm longer wheelbase to address rear legroom concerns and the introduction of the new Duratorq diesel engine.

**Production Timeline:**
- Launch: November 1, 2000
- Production End: August 1, 2007
- Model Years: 2001-2007
- Designer: Chris Bird (1997)

## Engine Technical Notes

There was a design flaw with the new 1.8 and 2.0L petrol units with the butterfly valves in the inlet manifold, which could cause severe engine damage when they failed. The plastic components deteriorated prematurely, potentially releasing metal and plastic debris into cylinders. Ford upgraded the part in late 2002 to early 2003, though random failures continued to occur on even upgraded components.

The Duratorq diesel represented a major improvement over the archaic Endura-D engine, featuring common rail technology and a clever turbine system that allows a certain amount of overboost, giving an extra 10% or so of torque for short periods.

## Interior Design

Ford dispensed with the rounded American style interior of the first generation, and developed a more sober, sophisticated, "Germanic" design, using more expensive materials. This reflected Ford's reaction to the benchmark set by the Volkswagen Passat (B5) in 1996, making it the first major American manufacturer to react to the new standard set by Volkswagen.

## Safety Features

### Active Safety
- Anti-lock brakes (all models)
- Electronic brake-force distribution (all models)
- Electronic stability program (ESP) - available as option

### Passive Safety

Ford implemented its "Intelligent Protection System" (IPS), using an "intelligent" array of sensors based on a neural network to optimize deployment of:
- Traditional front passenger airbags
- Side airbags
- Curtain airbags

The system determined the best combination of safety devices to deploy for a given crash situation.

### Additional Safety Equipment (2005)
- Seat Belt Warning System became standard with audible/visual reminder

### Euro NCAP Testing Results

Despite Ford's "One of the safest places to be" marketing claim, Euro NCAP's testing of the 2000 to 2007 Mondeo found that it protected worse than most key rivals, achieving only a lower-end 4 star rating.

Rivals tested included:
- Vauxhall Vectra
- Citroen C5
- Toyota Avensis
- Volkswagen Passat

The fundamental issue was traced to platform limitations: the first and second generation Mondeo were based on the relatively dated CDW27 platform, designed in the late 1980s. Higher-than-average risk of chest injury to the driver in the frontal impact remained even after Ford redesigned and re-tested portions of the car.

## Regional Markets

### Europe

**Petrol Options:**
- 1.8L Duratec (110 PS, 125 PS, 131 PS SCi)
- 2.0L Duratec (145 PS)
- 2.5L Duratec 24v V6 (170 PS)
- 3.0L Duratec 30 V6 (204 PS, 226 PS ST220)

**Diesel Options:**
- 2.0L Duratorq I4 (90 PS TDDi, 116 PS TDDi/TDCi, 131 PS TDCi)
- 2.2L Duratorq I4 (155 PS TDCi)

### North America - Mexico

In Mexico, the model was similar to the European version to replace the Ford Contour.
- Trim levels: Core, Edge, Ghia, and 100 units of ST220 (limited edition)
- Engine options: 2.0L 4-cylinder, 2.5L V6, 3.0L V6 (ST220 only)
- No turbodiesel or estate versions available
- Primary rivals: Chevrolet Vectra and Volkswagen Passat
- Final year: 2007, sold alongside Ford Fusion until replacement

### South America - Brazil

Ford do Brasil re-imported the second-generation Mondeo from 2002 until 2006, when the Fusion replaced it.
- Configuration: Saloon only
- Engine: 2.0L with 143 hp inline-4 only
- V6 version: Not available in this generation
- Trim: Single "Ghia" trim level
- Transmission: Manual or automatic gearbox

### Asia - Taiwan

The second generation (CD132 model code) was produced in Zhongli, Taiwan from 2001 by Ford Lio Ho as the Ford Mondeo Metrostar.

**Design Differences from European Model:**
- 17-inch alloy wheels
- 14-inch brake disks
- Completely redesigned front end
- Restyled tail lamps
- Interior: Same as European model with Nappa leather
- IP: Imported from Germany

**2003 Facelift - Mondeo Metrostar A+:**
- Completely redesigned front end
- Restyled tail lamps

**Technical Specifications (Taiwan):**
- Production: 2001-2007
- Body: 4-door sedan only
- Engine options: 2.0L DOHC inline-4, 2.5L DOHC V6 (post-2003 models)

### Asia - China

After the Ford Mondeo Metrostar A+ facelift, Changan Ford began domestic production of the Ford Lio Ho-designed model as the "Ford Mondeo" in China.
- Initial pricing: Over 300,000 RMB, later reduced to 230,000 RMB

## Market Performance and Reception

### United Kingdom
The Mondeo established itself as Britain's most popular automobile in its class and held this position every year from 2001 onwards, though it has never come higher than sixth in the SMMT's official list of the top selling cars in the UK each year. In 2003 it came tenth in the list.

### Australia and New Zealand
- **Australia:** The second generation Mondeo was never sold in Australia.
- **New Zealand:** Voted Car of the Year in 2002 by the New Zealand Motoring Writers' Guild.

## Key Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths:**
- Larger than predecessor with improved rear legroom
- New Duratorq diesel engine addressing previous weaknesses
- Sophisticated, Germanic interior design setting new standards
- Class-leading handling and ride inherited from previous generation
- Multiple body style options (saloon, liftback, estate)

**Challenges:**
- Inlet manifold butterfly valve design flaw on petrol engines (1.8/2.0L)
- Euro NCAP crash test results lower than key competitors
- Platform age (CDW27 based on late-1980s Mazda GE platform)
- Declining segment popularity during production run
