## Introduction

The Ford Duratorq engine is the marketing name of a range of Ford diesel engines introduced in 2000. This family succeeded the Endura-D platform and became widely distributed across Ford, Jaguar, Land Rover, Volvo, and Mazda vehicles through 2020.

## DLD Engine Family (1.4L - 1.8L)

### 1.4L DLD-414 ("Tiger")

A 1.4L (1,399 cc) inline-four turbodiesel producing 50 kW (68 PS; 67 hp) at 4500 rpm and 160 Nm (118 lb-ft) at 2000 rpm.

**Variants:**
- **8-valve design**: Uses BorgWarner KP35 turbocharger without intercooler. Euro III compliant, upgraded with diesel particulate filter in 2006 for Euro IV compliance.
- **DOHC 16-valve**: Features intercooled variable-geometry turbocharger with Delphi DCR1400 common-rail injection. Production ceased in 2006 due to Euro IV incompatibility.

**Production Location**: Built in France, UK, and India

### 1.5L Variant

Introduced in 2012, closely derived from the 1.6-litre engine. Bore was reduced from 75 to 73.5 mm while stroke remained at 88.3 mm.

### 1.6L DLD-416 ("Tiger")

A 1.6L (1,560 cc) engine producing 90 or 110 hp (67 or 82 kW; 91 or 112 PS). Designed by Peugeot engineers on behalf of PSA and Ford.

**Applications:**
- Ford Fiesta (Mk5 & Mk6)
- Ford Fusion (Europe)
- Ford Focus (Mk2 & Mk3)
- Ford Mondeo Mk3
- Ford C-Max, S-MAX, Galaxy
- Mazda2, Mazda3, Mazda5
- Mini Cooper D
- Citroen C2, C3, C4, C5
- Peugeot 206, 207, 208, 307, 308
- Volvo C30, S40, V50, V40

### 1.8L DLD-418 ("Lynx")

A 1.8L (1,753 cc) intercooled common rail diesel engine completely unrelated to the 1.4/1.6 units. This represents a development of Ford's original 1.8 8v Endura-D engine.

**Evolution:**
- Original Endura-D was heavily revised and updated with a variable-vane turbocharger and a Delphi high-pressure common rail injection system and relaunched in 2001 as the "DuraTorq TDCi."
- The original engine was rebadged "DuraTorq TDDi."

**Applications:**
- Ford Focus Mk1 & Mk2
- Ford Mondeo Mk4
- Ford C-MAX, Galaxy, S-MAX
- Ford Transit Connect

## ZSD ("Puma") Engine Family (2.0L - 3.2L)

These engines were codenamed "Puma" during development and are produced at the company's Dagenham plant in east London.

### 2.0L ZSD-420 Specifications

**Initial Release (2000-2001):**
- Displacement: 2.0L (1,998 cc)
- Power: 115 PS (85 kW; 113 hp)
- Torque: 280 Nm (207 lb-ft)
- Configuration: Direct injection turbodiesel with 16-valve cylinder head
- Features: Twin chain-driven camshafts, variable-geometry turbocharger with overboost function

**TDCi Version (2001-2002):**
- Power: 130 PS (96 kW; 128 hp)
- Torque: 330 Nm (243 lb-ft)
- Fuel System: Delphi common rail injection ("Turbo Diesel Commonrail injection")
- TDDi designation applied to original unit

**TDCi Version (2002-2005):**
- Power: 115 PS (85 kW; 113 hp)
- Torque: 285 Nm (210 lb-ft)
- Turbocharger: Fixed geometry (replacing variable geometry)

**Budget Version (2005):**
- Power: 90 PS (66 kW; 89 hp)
- Torque: 280 Nm (207 lb-ft)
- Target: Fleet buyers

**Applications:**
- Ford Transit 2.0 TDDi & TDCi (75, 85, 100, 125 PS variants)
- Ford Mondeo 2.0 TD & TDDi (2000-2002)
- Ford Mondeo 2.0 TDCi 130 (2001-2007)
- Ford Mondeo 2.0 TDCi 115 (2002-2007)
- Ford Mondeo 2.0 TDCi 90 (2005-2007)
- Jaguar X-Type 2.0d (2003-2009)

## 2005 TDCi (PSA DW Based) Engines

Continuing the Ford/PSA diesel engine joint-venture, these engines are sold under the Duratorq TDCi name by Ford, and as the HDi by Citroen and Peugeot.

Ford and PSA extended their Diesel engine joint-venture in October, 1999, to include the V6 platform, with the initial agreement announced in September, 1998.

### 2.0L (PSA DW10 Based)

Released in the 2005 Ford Focus, and followed in the 2007 Mk IV Mondeo.

**Design Features:**
- 16-valve cylinder head
- Twin belt-driven camshafts (exhaust camshaft connected to cambelt; inlet camshaft indirectly linked via timing chain)
- Variable geometry turbocharger with overboost function
- Always includes intercooler

**Power Outputs:**
- 2005 Focus: 136 PS (100 kW; 134 hp)
- 2007 Mondeo: 130 PS or 140 PS (96 or 103 kW; 128 or 138 hp)
- 2010 Update: 163 PS (120 kW; 161 bhp) with 340 Nm (251 lb-ft)

**Applications:**
- Ford Focus C-Max (2003-present): 136 PS, 340 Nm
- Ford Focus (2005-present): 136 PS, 340 Nm
- Ford S-Max (2006-present): 130 PS or 140 PS, 340 Nm
- Ford Galaxy (2006-present): Multiple power variants
- Ford Galaxy (2015-present): 213 PS, 450 Nm
- Ford Mondeo (2007-2014): 140 PS, 320 Nm
- Ford Mondeo (2007-2010): 160 PS, 340 Nm
- Ford Mondeo (2010-2014): 163 PS, 340 Nm
- Ford Mondeo (2014-2018): 150 PS or 180 PS
- Ford Mondeo (2015-2018): 210 PS, 450 Nm
- Ford Kuga (2008-present): Multiple variants
- Peugeot 307, 407, 508, 607 HDi
- Volvo C30, C70, S40, V50, S80, V70

### 2.2L (PSA DW12 Based, 2008+)

Ford fitted this engine to the Mk IV Mondeo, soon following in the Ford S-Max and Ford Galaxy. Re-engineered and built by PSA in Tremery.

## V8 Engine Family

### AJD-V8 (3.6L)

The 3.6L V8 is built at Ford's engine plant in Dagenham, Essex. Twin-turbocharged diesel V8 producing 270 PS (199 kW; 266 hp) and 640 Nm (472 lb-ft). Production began in April 2006.

Closely related to the 2.7L V6 version (same bore & stroke). Designed at Ford Otosan's R&D Center in Gebze, Turkey and Ford's Dagenham Diesel Centre product development site, with input from Land Rover powertrain team.

### 4.4L Variant

A 4.4 litre variant producing 250 kW (340 PS; 335 hp) and 700 Nm (516 lb-ft), built at Ford's Chihuahua Engine plant in Mexico.

**Applications:**
- Land Rover Range Rover (2010-2020)
- Land Rover Range Rover Sport (2013-2020)

## Production End

The new EcoBlue diesel engine range, originally codenamed "Panther" and planned to be available in 2.0- and 1.5-litre variants, progressively replaced the Duratorq engines from 2016.
