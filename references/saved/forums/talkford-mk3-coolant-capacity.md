Archived: 2026-04-12

---

**whitt** (OP):
I am looking at doing my coolant antifreeze in a mk3 petrol 2.0 duratec mondeo.

Does anybody have an instruction manual or know what the exact capacity of the coolant system is?

 
---

**compuwiz_uk:**
8.1L of coolant. Needed for 2.0 engine. (8.3 for 1.8 )

You may not get that much in there as there may be some old coolant still lurking etc but it will be damm close either way.

When i changed mine i just unclipped the lowest pipe from the radiator and drained the coolant out before refitting and then steadily refilling and following the routine for bleeding airlocks which was basically steady revs and then higher revs for a while and then a few higher blips etc if memory serves me right.

**compuwiz_uk:**
Depends on your concentrate mix level.

I went 50/50 with mine using oem ford long service stuff and bought 5l of it but only used 4l which was enough to leave a dribble of topup too. The final l i still have somewhere and did come in useful as additional topup as my motas stat decided to pack in shortly after the coolant change so it had to be dropped again but collected this time and re-used spilling as little as possible.

If your thinning out the mix more then 2.5l should be ok - quick maths say ur aiming for about 33% mix there so long as the mix is still good enough for winter at that level etc.
